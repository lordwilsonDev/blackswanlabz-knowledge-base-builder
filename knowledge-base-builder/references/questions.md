# The interview

Ask one at a time. Wait for the answer. If they skip one, ask once more, then write UNKNOWN and go on.

1. **Who is it for?** (a) your own team, (b) new hires, (c) customers or clients, (d) the public, (e) just me. Also: how many people will read it, and what do they do all day?
2. **What should it answer?** "Name the five questions people keep asking you." Ask what they now answer over and over, and where that knowledge lives today (documents, chats, someone's head).
3. **What do you already have?** Documents, handbooks, SOPs, FAQs, policies. Ask for file paths or pasted text. Ask what is confidential and must stay out.
4. **Name and feel.** The name to show, a two-line tagline, and one sentence on what the organisation does. Any words they like or dislike.
5. **Images.** Ask for: a logo (png, svg, jpg or webp; a square or simple mark works best) and, if they want, photos of people (only with each person's okay). Ask if they own the rights. If they have none, the site uses a letter mark.
6. **Colors.** (a) black and white, (b) one accent color (ask for a hex like #1a5fb4, or a color word and you pick the hex), (c) match the logo.
7. **Sections.** Suggest 3 to 6 from their answers (for example About, How we work, Customers, Tools, People & HR). Let them rename, add, remove. Each section needs an owner.
8. **People.** Who owns pages and answers questions? Name, role, a one-line bio they wrote, and what to ask them about. At least one person.
9. **Access.** Who may open it, and where will it run (their own computer, a company server)? A shared password is set by default; ask if they want a specific one.
10. **Anything else?** What would make it useful on day one?

## Say it back (the specs)
Print: name, tagline, audience, sections with the pages you will create (title and one-line purpose each), people, logo and colors, which pages hold real content and which are TODO, what is left out because it is confidential. Ask: "Is this right? Anything to change?" Build only after a clear yes.
